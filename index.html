🎵 How It Works: The Four Dimensions of Sound

🎼
Value → Pitch

Colder or lower measurements produce deep, low tones; extreme heat or higher values rise to bright, high pitches.

🎻
Spread → Timbre

Uniform data produces a pure sine wave; high spatial variability introduces richer harmonics and sawtooth tones.

⏱️
Rate of Change → Tempo

Times of rapid transformation accelerate the rhythm; steady periods play at a calm, measured pace.

🔔
Thresholds → Alert Marker

Crossing critical climate thresholds (such as 38°C / 100°F) triggers an immediate percussive warning chime.
