HOW SOUND IS MADE
🎵 Value → Pitch

Low value = deep tone (C3). High value = bright tone (C6). Every note is a real data point.

🎻 Variability → Timbre

Uniform conditions = pure sine wave. High spatial chaos = rich sawtooth harmonics.

⏱ Change Rate → Tempo

Slow change = calm 60 BPM. Rapid anomaly = urgent 160 BPM pulse.

🚨 Thresholds → Alarm

Crossing critical limits triggers a layered alarm bell and screen flash.

🎙 Story Persona

Each story has its own synth: AM Flute · FM Drift · Crisis Distortion · Rain Pad · Wildfire Lead.

🌐 Longitude → Stereo Pan

Western regions pan left; Eastern regions pan right — adding spatial dimension.
