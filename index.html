▌ SELECT EARTH OBSERVATION STORY

Choose a NASA time series. Each story converts real Earth science observations into sound — pitch, timbre, tempo, and alert tones — synchronized with live NASA satellite imagery from GIBS.

⚙️ SONIFICATION MAPPING SPEC

🎼
VALUE → PITCH

Linear: low data = C3 (deep), high data = C6 (bright). Every note is a data point.

🎻
VARIABILITY → TIMBRE

Uniform conditions = pure sine. Chaotic variability = rich sawtooth harmonics.

⏱
CHANGE RATE → TEMPO

Stable periods = slow rhythm (60 BPM). Rapid anomalies = urgent fast pulse (160 BPM).

🚨
THRESHOLD → ALARM

Crossing critical limits triggers a layered bell + burst alert and a screen flash.

🔊
STORY PERSONA

Each story uses a unique synthesizer: AM Flute · FM Drift · Crisis Distortion.

🌐
LONGITUDE → STEREO PAN

West regions pan left channel; East regions pan right, adding spatial dimension.
