Scientific Mathematical Sonification Pipeline

Earth Jukebox rejects arbitrary artistic synth tracks. Every acoustic dimension is a mathematically reproducible transformation of physical NASA data.

OBSERVED VALUE → Continuous Pitch (MIDI 48 / C3, 130.8 Hz through MIDI 84 / C6, 1046.5 Hz)
SPATIAL VARIABILITY → Timbre Spectrum: Pure sine wave (uniform data) → Rich harmonic sawtooth (turbulent entropy)
CHANGE VELOCITY (Δ) → Dynamic Playback Pace: 60 BPM (stable climate periods) → 160 BPM (rapid anomalous swings)
GEOGRAPHIC LONGITUDE → Spatial Stereo Pan: 180°W (Full Left) → Prime Meridian (Center) → 180°E (Full Right)
EXTREME THRESHOLDS → Critical Multi-Phonic Alarm: Layered dissonant chime burst + high-contrast visual flash
